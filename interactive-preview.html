How values are calculated

Economic cost = points × reference CPP ÷ 100 + USD award fees.

Realized CPP = (cash fare − USD award fees) ÷ points × 100.

Economic savings = cash fare − economic cost.

Example: 80,000 points at 1.30¢ + $6 fees = $1,046 economic cost. A $4,150 cash fare produces 5.18¢ CPP and $3,104 economic savings.

Cash fare, economic cost, savings, and realized CPP remain separate measures.
